# Ⓓ English lessons site (mr-dilshod.github.io)

- Lessons are `lesson-N.html` (audio in `lesson-N/`). Each file starts itself with `EWD.start({number, title: "Unit: Lesson", meta: "Own It! 1 · Unit · Student's Book p. X", chips: [...]})`.
- `lessons.json` is generated, never edited by hand: `.github/workflows/lessons.yml` runs `tools/build_lessons.py` on every push that touches a lesson file or `worksheets.json`.
- The blog page https://sajqaliruizamin.blogspot.com/p/english.html reads `lessons.json`, so a pushed lesson appears there automatically.
- Worksheet links (Bilimlink) go in `worksheets.json` as `"N": "https://bilimlink.uz/share/..."`. When a new lesson or worksheet arrives, add its entry there in the same push.
- Worksheets are gated: the link is NOT put in `lessons.json` or on the blog. The lesson's end screen (engine `worksheetBox`) fetches `worksheets.json` and shows "Open the worksheet" only after the homework photo is sent. Mr. Dilshod gives each worksheet to the Bilimlink group with the Telegram announcement unticked and does not announce worksheets in Telegram at all. Students' personal Bilimlink pages (bilimlink.uz/p/...) list group worksheets with an "Open" status label only (no link into the worksheet), so they don't bypass the gate.
- Highlighted words (`<span class='en'>`) are tappable: each lesson's LESSON object needs `words` with uz/ru/tg meanings for every highlighted word or phrase (whole word when only part is coloured); the end screen lists them under "Words to learn". Colour only the lesson's new words, the grammar forms it teaches and its Useful language phrases (usually 10–25); use `<b>` for stressed letters, endings and other emphasis.
- Slide text, word lists and English in voice-note scripts are American English (color, favorite, Mr., period, soccer, July 24th). Book pictures stay British, so typed answers also accept the book's British form.
- The unit name is the part of `title` before the colon, so keep titles as `"Unit 1: Vocabulary"`-style.
- Phonics pages (eshit-va-takrorla, chalkashtirma, iliq-h-tovushi, slushay-i-povtoryay, ne-pereputay, teplyy-zvuk-h, uzun-o, dlinnoe-o, th-tovushi, zvuk-th, ortiqcha-tovush-yoq, bez-lishnikh-zvukov) carry `<meta name="ver" content="…">`. A browser's old copy fetches the page fresh, compares `ver` and reloads itself. Change `ver` on every edit of those pages, or the old copies stay.
- Learn to Read (phonics) sheets; the series is always called "Learn to Read" (never "Reading", "Phonics" or "Learn to read English"). Sheets are titled by step: `<title>N qadam: Name</title>` for Uzbek (e.g. "1 qadam: Eshit va takrorla") and `<title>Шаг N: Name</title>` for Russian (e.g. "Шаг 1: Слушай и повторяй"). New sheets take the next step number in their language; add them to the phonics list above and as a card on the blog's Learn to Read page (address stays p/reading.html).
- Lessons open in order: a lesson not started yet opens only if the previous lesson was passed with more than 50% (engine `askPassed`/`passedHere`). The phone remembers a pass (`dee-pass-N`); otherwise the lesson asks the Drive web app (`doGet?check=1&lesson=N&name=…`, names compared without case, punctuation or word order). Lesson 1 is always open; a lesson already started is never locked; if the web app can't answer (old code), the student is let in.
- Lesson photo buttons (engine `blockPhoto`) have NO `capture` (it made Android close Chrome and lose the photo); students take or choose a photo. `photo.html` keeps `capture`. Every photo sends `info` (camera name from EXIF, size, file age; "⚠ check" for likely AI pictures, screenshots or old files) to the Check column of the Photos and Homework tabs. Keep both when editing the engine or `photo.html`.
- Uzbek voice notes say "izquvar" (never "detektiv"); AI is "sunʼiy idrok" (uz), "искусственный интеллект" (ru), "мағзи маслуӣ" (tg). Tajik "well done" is «Баракалло».
